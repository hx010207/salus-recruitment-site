ABOUT SALUS

Salus Cybersecurity Club is a dynamic student-led initiative from the Department of Cybersecurity at Jain (Deemed-to-be University), FET, Kanakapura Road Campus. The club is dedicated to fostering technical excellence and professional growth in cybersecurity and related domains.

We provide a structured environment where students develop practical skills in ethical hacking, network security, secure coding, digital forensics, and threat analysis through workshops, hands-on labs, competitions, and real-world projects.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry practices by encouraging applied learning, collaboration, and innovation. Salus focuses on building disciplined, industry-ready cybersecurity professionals who value responsibility, teamwork, and continuous learning.

We also collaborate with organizations and industry experts to strengthen the cybersecurity ecosystem, creating opportunities for students to gain exposure, mentorship, networking, and career growth.
